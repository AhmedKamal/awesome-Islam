# Trusted Islamic Websites

A classified listing of trustworthy Islamic websites, originally based on
[bilalphilips.com](https://bilalphilips.com/) (the source page no longer
exists) and cleaned up since.

> **Last verified: September 2026.** Dead, parked, and hijacked domains were
> removed, moved sites were updated to their current URLs, and the old
> numeric ratings were dropped — they dated back to the original ~2005 review
> and can no longer be re-checked. Descriptions below are condensed from those
> original reviews; references to long-gone technology (floppy disks, Flash,
> dial-up) were removed.

The original reviewers rated sites on: authenticity and closeness to the
Qur'an, Sunnah, and understanding of the Salaf; quality of language and
sourcing (with hadith verification); quantity of material; free downloadable
audio, video, and e-books; and update frequency. Sites with a general name
were expected to cover many aspects of Islam; sites with a focused name were
judged on how well they cover their stated aim.

## Qur'an: study, translation, and recitation

- [King Fahd Complex (qurancomplex.gov.sa)](https://qurancomplex.gov.sa/) —
  Qur'an translations in many languages, quality recitations for download,
  and a small searchable fatwa database.
- [Understand Quran](https://understandquran.com/) — "Understand the Qur'an
  the easy way": courses, word-for-word translations, Qur'an dictionaries in
  several languages, and beginner Tajweed material.
- [eMuslim](http://www.emuslim.com/) — Qur'an word lists and word-for-word
  dictionaries plus articles.
- [About Tajweed](http://www.abouttajweed.com/) — systematic Tajweed rules
  for Hafs 'an 'Asim, with a Q&A archive.
- [Sabbir.com](https://sabbir.com/) — audio of the English Qur'an translation
  alongside Arabic recitation, ayah by ayah.

## Fatwa and Q&A

- [IslamQA](https://islamqa.info/) — huge, categorized, searchable fatwa
  archive supervised by Sheikh Muhammad Salih al-Munajjid, with references
  and e-books. (Previously islam-qa.com.)
- [Fatwa Online](https://www.fatwa-online.com/) — fatwas, lectures, and
  downloadable e-book compilations, plus audio biographies of contemporary
  scholars.
- [IslamWeb](https://www.islamweb.net/en/) — articles, books, audios, and
  dawah material with a polished layout.
- [IslamToday](http://islamtoday.com/) — e-books, fatwa archives with
  question submission, and Qur'an/hadith showcases.

## Arabic language

- [Madinah Arabic](https://madinaharabic.com/) — free, structured courses
  teaching Arabic on the Madinah Book series, with a student forum.

## Da'wah and new Muslims

- [WhyIslam](https://www.whyislam.org/) — ICNA dawah site: toll-free line,
  email, Q&A, forum, articles, and free literature ordering.
- [Islam Guide](https://www.islam-guide.com/) — a single excellent dawah
  book as a website (scientific proofs of the Qur'an), downloadable as PDF
  in several languages.
- [Shariah Way](https://www.shariahway.com/) — article-heavy site serving
  new Muslims, with a basics quiz and mailing list.
- [IslamInfo & Qur'an Speaks & Shabir Ally](https://islaminfo.com/new/) —
  partner sites around Shabbir Ally's dawah work, with online courses and
  instant-scoring tests ([Qur'an Speaks](https://www.quranspeaks.com/),
  [shabirally.com](https://shabirally.com/)).
- [Islam Tomorrow](https://www.islamtomorrow.com/) — dawah articles and
  comparative material.
- [Muhammad.net](http://muhammad.net/) — a full site on Prophet Muhammad
  (peace be upon him), aimed mainly at newcomers, with general Islamic and
  Qur'an material.
- [Islam 101](http://islam101.com/) — educational site on Islam, civilization,
  and contemporary issues, with online courses and instant-scoring tests.

## History, seerah, and civilization

- [Muslim Heritage](http://muslimheritage.com/) — Muslim contributions to
  science, technology, and civilization, with personalities, cities, and
  discoveries.
- [Imaan.net](http://imaan.net/) — Faith Network site.
- [Seerah.net](http://seerah.net/) — seerah resources.
- [Crusades.org](http://crusades.org/) — the Crusades from an Islamic and
  historical perspective, including Salahuddin's service to Islam.
- [Gospel of Barnabas](https://barnabas.net/) — the Gospel text with Islamic
  commentary, downloadable for dawah use.
- [Sahaba.net](http://sahaba.net/) — dedicated to the Sahaba (may Allah be
  pleased with them), with stories and a forum.

## Creed, methodology, and libraries

- [Salafi Manhaj](http://salafimanhaj.com/) — e-books, fatwas, and audio.
- [Understand Islam](https://www.understand-islam.net/) — authentic material
  including translated works of Sheikh al-Uthaymeen and a study program with
  tests.
- [Islam House](https://islamhouse.com/en) — large multilingual store of
  e-books, lectures, and Qur'an translations.
- [IslamWay](https://en.islamway.net/) — huge variety of articles, e-books,
  lectures, and downloadable Qur'an audio with English translation.
- [Islaam.net](http://www.islaam.net/main/) — articles and lectures with an
  excellent collection of Qur'an recitations by various reciters.
- [Islam Basics](https://islambasics.com/) — e-library with many free
  downloadable books in multiple formats and full-text search across them.
- [QSS (Quran and Sunnah Society)](https://qss.org/) — authentic material,
  including Sheikh al-Albani's prayer book online.
- [Ahya.org](http://www.ahya.org/) — articles, e-books, and lectures.
- [Real Islam](http://www.realislam.com/) — average but sound introductory
  material.
- [The Clear Path](http://www.theclearpath.com/) — articles on Islamic
  methodology; small but clean and fast.
- [Salim Morgan (java-man.com)](http://www.java-man.com/) — quality English
  translations of classical Arabic books.
- [Jaafar Idris](https://www.jaafaridris.com/) — writings and lectures of
  Sheikh Jaafar Idris.

## Refutation and comparative studies

- [Twelver Shia](http://www.twelvershia.net/) — academic refutations of
  Shiism with audio and translated books.
- [Mahajjah](https://mahajjah.com/) — English translations of books against
  Shia propaganda, unavailable elsewhere online.
- [Nahjul Balagha](http://nahjul-balagha.net/) — examining the sermons
  attributed to Ali (may Allah be pleased with him) through hadith
  criticism.
- [Shia Scans](https://shiascans.com/) — refutations with scans from Shia
  books and brief commentaries.

## Khutbahs

- [Al-Minbar](http://www.alminbar.com/) — large database of quality khutbahs,
  useful as articles too (note: hadith references are not always provided).

## Audio and multimedia libraries

- [Audio Islam](https://audioislam.com/) — large collection of beneficial
  Islamic audio lectures.
- [Aswat al-Islam](http://www.aswatalislam.net/) — unrivalled quantity of
  audio and video; note the original reviewers' caution that authentic and
  inauthentic speakers are bundled together.
- [Islam Channel](https://islamchannel.tv/) — Islamic satellite channel with
  articles and an online fatwa facility.

## Link collections

- [Al-Sunnah.com](http://www.al-sunnah.com/) — collection of links to good
  content elsewhere, complementing link pages below.
- [Sultan.org](http://www.sultan.org/) — patiently compiled Islamic links
  page.

## Community and local

- [Muhammadiyah Singapore](https://muhammadiyah.org.sg/) — articles from a
  Singaporean Muslim organization (deep article URLs changed; start from the
  homepage).
- [IIIE (Institute of Islamic Information and Education)](http://www.iiie.net/) —
  North-American dawah brochures and correct basic information on Islam.
